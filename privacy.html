Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what we do with your information when you get a website built by PixelForge. Simple language — no legal jargon.

What we collect

When you fill the order form: your name, email, WhatsApp number, city, portfolio details (bio, skills, projects, social links), and your CV/resume and profile photo if provided.

Why we collect this

For one reason only — to build your website. Your bio, skills, and photo are directly used in your portfolio website. Contact details are used to send you your demo and updates.

Do we share your data?

No. Your data is not shared with any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or other clients. Only our team, who is building your website, has access to it.

Where is the data stored

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (a cloud database) with row-level security — meaning no one but admins can see your complete details.

How to request deletion

After your website is live, if you want us to delete your CV, photo, or any other data, just let us know on WhatsApp — we will delete it within 48 hours.

Cookies

We only save essential things — like your theme (dark/light) preference in your browser's local storage. We do not use any tracking or advertising cookies.

Questions?

If you have any confusion, just ask us directly on WhatsApp or email — we'll be happy to answer.
